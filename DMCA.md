# Spartan Gaming™ DMCA Takedown Policy

**Effective date:** August 17, 2026
**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

Spartan Software Enterprises respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of Spartan Gaming™ to do the same. This policy describes how we handle Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) takedown notices and counter-notifications related to the Spartan Gaming open-source project.

---

## 1. About Spartan Gaming

Spartan Gaming is an open-source, local-first gaming frontend. It does not host, stream, store, or distribute any copyrighted content. All provider content, game streams, and media accessed through Spartan Gaming are served directly by third-party providers or the user's own devices. Spartan Gaming acts solely as a local interface.

## 3. Filing a DMCA takedown notice

If you believe that content accessible through or related to Spartan Gaming infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice to our designated agent at the email address above.

Your notice must include substantially the following information as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

1. **Physical or electronic signature** of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
2. **Identification of the copyrighted work** claimed to have been infringed, or if multiple copyrighted works are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works.
3. **Identification of the material** that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, and information reasonably sufficient to permit Spartan Software Enterprises to locate the material (such as a URL, file path, or description).
4. **Contact information** of the complaining party, including name, address, telephone number, and email address.
5. **A statement that the complaining party has a good faith belief** that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. **A statement that the information in the notification is accurate**, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is the copyright owner or is authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Incomplete notices may not be processed.

## 4. Processing takedown notices

Upon receiving a valid DMCA takedown notice, Spartan Software Enterprises will:

- Review the notice for completeness and validity.
- Take reasonable steps to remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material within the Spartan Gaming project repository or distribution channels, where applicable.
- Notify the affected user, if identifiable, that their content has been removed or access has been disabled, along with a copy of the takedown notice and information about how to file a counter-notification.

Because Spartan Gaming is a local-first application that does not host provider content, takedown notices related to third-party provider content, game streams, or media should be directed to the relevant provider. Spartan Software Enterprises can only act on material that it actually hosts or distributes, such as source code, documentation, or project assets.

## 5. Filing a counter-notification

If your content has been removed or disabled as a result of a DMCA takedown notice and you believe the removal was based on a mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification with our designated agent.

Your counter-notification must include substantially the following information as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3):

1. **Physical or electronic signature** of the user who submitted the counter-notification.
2. **Identification of the material** that has been removed or to which access has been disabled, and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed or access was disabled.
3. **A statement under penalty of perjury** that the user has a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material.
4. **Name, address, and telephone number** of the user, and a statement that the user consents to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which the address is located, or if outside the United States, to the jurisdiction of the United States District Court for the Northern District of California, and that the user will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification or an agent of that person.

## 6. Repeat infringers

Spartan Software Enterprises reserves the right to terminate access to the Spartan Gaming project for users who are determined to be repeat infringers under the DMCA or applicable intellectual property law.

## 7. Good-faith disputes

Spartan Software Enterprises encourages good-faith communication between parties before escalating to formal DMCA proceedings. Many disputes can be resolved by direct communication between the copyright holder and the content provider.

## 8. Limitations

This policy applies to the Spartan Gaming project repository, distribution channels, and documentation maintained by Spartan Software Enterprises. It does not apply to third-party provider services, game streams, or content hosted by providers or on user-owned devices, which are outside Spartan Software Enterprises' control.
